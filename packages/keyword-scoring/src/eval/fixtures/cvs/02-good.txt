PROFILE
I've spent five years in charity shops, starting out as a volunteer myself, so I know what it's like to walk in on day one not knowing where anything goes. These days most of my time goes on finding, training and keeping volunteers, and I get a real buzz from watching someone shy turn into the person who runs the till on a Saturday. Housing matters to me personally - I've watched friends sofa-surf for months in this city - and I'd love my work to feed into that.
